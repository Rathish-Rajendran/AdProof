# Frequently asked questions

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all product information here are fictional.

## Water exposure

No. The AB-01 is splash-resistant for everyday use (IPX4), not waterproof, and should not be submerged.

## How long does its battery last?

Up to 30 hours with ANC off and up to 22 hours with ANC on. A 10-minute charge gives up to 3 hours of playback.

## What is included?

The AB-01 has a 1-year limited warranty and a 30-day return period subject to the applicable terms.
