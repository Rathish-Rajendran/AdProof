# Audio product troubleshooting guide

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all company information here are fictional.

## Start with a quick check

Confirm that the product is charged, the volume is audible, and the audio source is playing. Turn Bluetooth off and on at the source device, then reconnect the AeroBeat product from the device's Bluetooth menu. If more than one saved device is nearby, temporarily disconnect the one that is not in use.

Keep the product and audio source within normal listening range while checking the connection. Nearby wireless devices or physical obstructions can affect a connection. Restarting the source device can clear a temporary connection state.

## Charging questions

Use the product's USB charging connection and inspect the cable and connector for visible damage or debris. Try a compatible cable and power source. Do not force a connector into place. If the product does not show a charging indicator, disconnect it, check the cable, and try again with a known-working power source.

Charging time and playback duration depend on the product and its use. For model-specific battery information, consult the relevant product page. The AB-01 battery figures include usage conditions; they should not be generalized to the other products.

## Audio interruptions

If audio cuts out, move the source device closer and pause other active wireless connections. Check whether the interruption happens with more than one app or audio source. Reconnecting the product can help determine whether the issue is with the source or the headset.

For an uneven fit or low volume in one earbud, reseat the AB-02 Pro earbuds and inspect the tips for obstruction. For the Mini speaker, verify that the audio source is connected to the intended speaker and that its output is enabled.

## Active noise cancellation

The AB-01 and AB-02 Pro include active noise cancellation. Confirm the selected listening mode in the product controls or connected-device settings. A mode change can alter the listening experience and battery duration. The Mini speaker is a speaker product and does not use the headphone ANC figures.

## When to contact support

Stop using a product if it is unusually hot, visibly damaged, or has a damaged charging connection. Do not open the enclosure. Contact support with the product name, order reference, a description of the symptom, and the troubleshooting steps already tried. Support can direct the customer to current service and purchase terms.
