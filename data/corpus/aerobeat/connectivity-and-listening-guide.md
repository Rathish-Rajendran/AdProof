# Connectivity and listening guide

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all company information here are fictional.

## Bluetooth setup

Each AeroBeat wireless product can be selected from a compatible source device's Bluetooth settings. Keep the source nearby during pairing, select the product name from the available-device list, and wait for the connection confirmation. When changing source devices, disconnect from the currently connected device first.

If the product does not appear, check that it is powered on, then refresh the Bluetooth list. Remove a stale saved pairing from the source device and retry. Restarting Bluetooth or the source device can clear a temporary connection state.

## Selecting an audio output

Some phones, computers, and media applications remember a previously used output. Check the active output in both the device settings and the application. If one app has no sound, test another app before changing product settings.

For a call, review the call application's microphone and speaker selection. For media playback, review the media output. A call and a music app can use different audio settings even when the same AeroBeat product is connected.

## Connection interruptions

Move the source device closer to the product and reduce nearby obstructions. Temporarily disconnect unused Bluetooth devices and test with one active source. If the connection remains intermittent, compare the behavior with another source device and record which apps are affected.

## Choosing the right guide

AB-01 pairing and battery steps are described in its dedicated support pages. The AB-02 Pro guide covers earbud fit and case charging. The Mini speaker guide covers speaker pairing and care. Use the product-specific page for battery figures, water-resistance conditions, and model-specific connections.

## Contact support

If reconnection steps do not resolve the issue, provide the product name, source-device type, and the sequence of steps attempted. Include whether the issue affects calls, media, or both. Support can use this information to direct the customer to the appropriate product instructions.
