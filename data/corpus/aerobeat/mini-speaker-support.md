# AeroBeat Mini speaker setup and care

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all product information here are fictional.

## Pairing and playback

Charge the Mini speaker, turn it on, and enable Bluetooth on the source device. Select AeroBeat Mini from the available-device list. Keep the source close during setup. If the speaker is already connected to another device, disconnect that device before starting a new pairing.

If sound is missing, check the source device's output selection and volume, then reconnect the speaker. Try a different audio app or source device to narrow down whether the issue is with the speaker or the source.

## Battery and charging

The Mini speaker provides up to 12 hours of battery life. Actual playback time depends on settings and use. Use a compatible charging cable and check both the connector and power source if charging does not begin.

## Water and storage

The Mini speaker has an IP67 rating and can be submerged in fresh water to 1 m for up to 30 minutes. It is not intended for salt water. Rinse and dry the exterior as directed by the product care instructions after exposure; do not charge the speaker while it is wet.

## Getting help

When contacting support, include the product name, the source device, and whether the speaker powers on and pairs. Provide the order reference when asking about a purchase.
