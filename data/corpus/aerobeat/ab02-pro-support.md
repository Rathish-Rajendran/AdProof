# AeroBeat AB-02 Pro setup and care

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all product information here are fictional.

## Pairing

Charge the earbuds and case before first use. Enable Bluetooth on the source device, open its saved-device settings, and select AeroBeat AB-02 Pro. Keep the source close while pairing. If the earbuds do not appear, turn Bluetooth off and on, remove an old saved entry if present, and try again.

## Battery

The AB-02 Pro provides up to 8 hours of use from the earbuds and up to 32 hours with the charging case. Actual playback time depends on settings and use. For the case and earbuds, follow the charging indicators described in the product guide.

## Fit and cleaning

Choose an ear tip that feels secure without discomfort. Inspect the tips and sound openings for debris, and clean the exterior with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use sharp tools on the speaker openings.

## Water resistance and care

The AB-02 Pro has an IPX5 sweat- and water-resistance rating and is not for swimming. Dry the product before charging and follow the product page's care instructions.

## Support

If pairing, charging, or audio problems continue, note the source-device model, the status of the charging case, and the steps already tried. Contact support with the order reference for further help.
