# AeroBeat customer service guide

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all company information here are fictional.

## Finding the right help

Use the product-specific support page for setup, connectivity, charging, and care questions. The company support index also includes order tracking, shipping, returns, and general product questions. Product pages list the current model specifications and prices.

## Preparing a support request

Include the product name and a short description of the issue. For an order question, provide the order reference and the email address used at checkout. For a technical issue, describe when it began, which source device is involved, and the steps already tried. This information helps support route the request.

Do not send payment-card numbers, passwords, or other account credentials in a support message. If a support reply asks for order details, use the official support channel and share only the information needed to locate the order.

## Common request types

For a Bluetooth question, identify the source device and whether the AeroBeat product appears in its saved-device list. For charging questions, note the cable and power source used and whether a charging indicator appeared. For delivery questions, include the tracking status and carrier reference. For a return question, consult the published returns terms and include the order reference.

## Product care

Keep products and charging connectors clean and dry according to the relevant product instructions. Do not open a product enclosure or force a cable connector. Stop using a damaged product and ask support for the current service options.

## Policies and updates

Warranty and return coverage is governed by the published terms that apply to the purchase. Support articles explain general steps and do not replace those terms. If a support page and a product page differ, check the current product listing or contact support for clarification.

## Follow-up

Keep the support case reference with the order information. If the problem continues after following the recommended steps, reply to the existing case with the new observations rather than creating duplicate requests. Support may ask for additional details about the product or troubleshooting already completed.
