# AeroBeat AB-01 retailer fact sheet

Regular price: $80.

Current price: $60.

Discount: 25% off the regular price.

The AB-01 is splash-resistant for everyday use and has an IPX4 rating. It is not waterproof and should not be submerged.

Wireless connection: Bluetooth 5.3. Charging connection: USB-C. Weight: 250 g.
