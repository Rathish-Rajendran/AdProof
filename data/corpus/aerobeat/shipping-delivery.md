# Order, shipping, and delivery information

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all company information here are fictional.

## Before an order ships

Customers should review the selected product, quantity, shipping address, and payment details before submitting an order. The order confirmation summarizes the items and destination. If an address needs correction, contact support promptly; a submitted change may not be possible once the parcel enters carrier processing.

## Tracking a shipment

When tracking is available, the order page or shipping notification provides the carrier reference. Carrier scans may take time to appear after handoff. The tracking page is the best place to check the latest movement and delivery estimate. An estimate is provided by the carrier and may change as the parcel moves through its network.

## Delivery questions

Check that the shipping address and any delivery instructions are current. If tracking shows a delivery scan but the parcel is not visible, check the usual drop-off location and ask other household members before contacting support. If the tracking status has not updated, allow time for the next carrier scan and then share the order reference with support.

For a parcel marked as delayed, use the carrier's tracking page for its latest estimate. AeroBeat support can help customers locate the order record and explain the available next steps. Delivery dates and carrier handling are not product specifications.

## Returns and order changes

The AB-01 and Mini speaker product information lists 30-day returns, subject to the published terms. The AB-02 Pro page directs customers to the same current returns information. Review the terms for eligibility and process details before sending an item back. This page does not replace the warranty or returns terms.

## Contacting support

Include the order reference, the email address used for purchase, and a brief description of the question. Avoid sending payment-card details in a support message. Support can use the order reference to check the order and direct the customer to the relevant carrier or returns information.
