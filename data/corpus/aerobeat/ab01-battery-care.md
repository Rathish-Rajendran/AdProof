# AeroBeat AB-01 charging and battery support

This is a synthetic demonstration document; AeroBeat and all product information here are fictional.

## Charging the headphones

Connect a USB-C charging cable to the AB-01 and to a compatible power source. Check that both ends are seated and that the cable is not damaged. Keep the connector dry and clear of debris. Disconnect the cable by holding the plug rather than pulling on the wire.

If charging does not begin, try another compatible cable and power source. Inspect the port under good light without inserting a sharp object. If the charging indicator still does not appear, stop troubleshooting and contact support.

## Battery information

The AB-01 provides up to 30 hours with active noise cancellation (ANC) off and up to 22 hours with ANC on. A 10-minute charge provides up to 3 hours of playback. Battery life depends on settings and use, including volume, connected features, and listening conditions.

These figures describe the AB-01 only. For AB-02 Pro and Mini speaker battery information, see their own product pages. A quick-charge figure and a full-charge playback duration describe different conditions.

## Keeping the product in good condition

Store the headphones in a dry place and avoid leaving them in extreme heat. Clean the exterior with a soft, dry cloth. Do not submerge the AB-01; its splash resistance is limited to everyday use as stated on the product page.

## Support

When contacting support, include the product model, what charging source was used, whether an indicator appeared, and when the problem began. The published warranty terms explain coverage and service options.
